# Native Gallery Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 30, 2026

Native Gallery is designed to organize and protect photos and videos primarily
on your Android device. The application does not require an account and does
not operate a developer-controlled cloud service.

## Information handled by Native Gallery

With your permission, Native Gallery accesses photos, videos, and related media
metadata stored on your device so it can display, search, organize, edit, share,
delete, and protect them. Document recognition and text search use the bundled
Google ML Kit text-recognition model on the device. The selected image inputs,
recognized text, and OCR results remain on the device and are not sent to
Google or the developer.

Locked-media files and the information required to manage them are stored in
the app's private storage and protected using Android Keystore-backed
encryption. Native Gallery can use Android's biometric prompt to unlock
protected media. The app does not receive or store your fingerprint, face
template, or other biometric credentials; Android reports only whether
authentication succeeded.

## Technical data collected by ML Kit

Native Gallery includes the Google ML Kit Android SDK. According to Google's
[ML Kit data disclosure](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure),
the SDK collects limited technical data for diagnostics and usage analytics:

- device manufacturer, model, operating-system version/build, and available ML
  hardware accelerators;
- application package name and version;
- a per-installation identifier not intended to identify a person or physical
  device;
- performance metrics such as latency;
- API configuration such as image format and resolution;
- feature input/output sizes and feature version; and
- events and error codes for initialization, detection, model handling, and
  resource release.

Google states that this technical data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS and
is not transferred to third parties. It is handled under Google's
[API Terms of Service](https://developers.google.com/terms) and applicable
Google privacy terms. Native Gallery does not include a separate developer
analytics or advertising SDK.

## Sharing

User-initiated sharing is handled by Android and sends only the items you select
to the destination you choose. Native Gallery does not sell media, recognized
text, vault contents, or personal information.

## Permissions

Photo and video permissions are used to provide gallery functionality. Android
may separately request confirmation before Native Gallery modifies or deletes
media created by another app. On supported Android versions, you may optionally
enable Android's special media-management access to reduce repeated confirmation
screens; disabling it preserves the normal system confirmation flow. Biometric
permission is used only when you enable biometric vault access.

You can revoke permissions at any time in Android Settings. Some gallery
features will stop working when the related permission is revoked.

## Retention and deletion

The developer does not store your media or vault data. You control media
deletion through the app and Android. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling
it removes app-private settings and locked-media data; uninstalling does not
delete ordinary photos and videos that remain in shared device storage. Restore
any locked items you want to keep before clearing storage or uninstalling.

Android backups are disabled for Native Gallery. ML Kit's technical diagnostic
data is retained according to Google's applicable terms and privacy practices.

## Children and policy changes

Native Gallery is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect
children's personal information. If this policy changes, the effective date
above will be updated and the revised policy will be published with the app.
